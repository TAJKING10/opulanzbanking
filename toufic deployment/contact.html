Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does it take to open an account?

+

Personal accounts can be opened in under 5 minutes online. Business accounts typically take 1–3 business days after document verification. Company accounts for newly formed entities may take 3–5 days depending on KYB requirements.

What documents do I need for KYC verification?

+

For individuals: a valid government-issued photo ID (passport or national ID), proof of address (utility bill or bank statement less than 3 months old), and a selfie. For businesses, we require company registration documents, beneficial ownership information, and ID for each director/shareholder.

Is my money protected?

+

Yes. Funds held in Opulanz accounts are protected under the French Fonds de Garantie des Dépôts et de Résolution (FGDR) up to €100,000 per depositor. Your deposits are fully segregated and held at licensed European credit institutions.

Can I access Opulanz services from outside France/Luxembourg?

+

Yes. We serve clients in 15+ countries. As long as you are not from a sanctioned jurisdiction, you can open accounts, access tax advisory, and use investment services remotely. Some services may be restricted based on your country of residence.

What are your advisory fees?

+

We operate on a transparent fee structure with no hidden charges. Investment advisory fees range from 0.5% to 1.2% AUM annually. Tax advisory is billed hourly or at a fixed project rate. Banking and accounting fees are disclosed upfront. Book a free consultation for a personalized quote.

How do I qualify for SPV investment access?

+

SPV investments are reserved for qualified investors under EU regulations: individuals with a financial portfolio exceeding €500,000 and at least one year of professional experience in the financial sector, or institutional investors. Minimum investment is typically €100,000 per SPV.
