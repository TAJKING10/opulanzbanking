Our Story

A Platform Born from Experience

Founded in Paris in 2018, Opulanz grew out of a simple observation: high-quality financial services — banking, tax planning, investment management — were fragmented, expensive, and reserved for the ultra-wealthy. We set out to change that.

Our founding team brings together 40+ years of combined experience across investment banking, regulatory compliance, tax law, and technology. We built the platform we always wished existed — comprehensive, transparent, and fully regulated.

Today we serve over 12,000 clients across France, Luxembourg, and 15+ countries, managing more than €2 billion in assets and processing thousands of company formations annually.

Compliance & Licensing

Fully Regulated & Licensed

Opulanz operates under the strictest financial regulatory frameworks in Europe. Your money and data are protected.

ACPR

Autorité de Contrôle Prudentiel et de Résolution

France's primary banking and insurance supervisor. Our banking and payment services are fully licensed and supervised by ACPR, ensuring prudential standards and consumer protection.

AMF

Autorité des Marchés Financiers

The French financial markets authority regulates our investment advisory and portfolio management services. We operate under MiFID II, providing best-execution and suitability guarantees.

CSSF

Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier

Luxembourg's financial sector regulator oversees our Luxembourg operations including company formation, SPV structures, and alternative investment services.

🔒

GDPR & Data Security

Full GDPR compliance, 256-bit AES encryption, ISO 27001 certified infrastructure, and two-factor authentication on all accounts. Your privacy is non-negotiable.
